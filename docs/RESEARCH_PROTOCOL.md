# Research and reporting protocol

Record the hypothesis before each new experiment. Keep a fixed control, matched data and compute, independent seeds or folds, the official metric and per-run records. Preserve successful and unsuccessful results, exceptions, and incomplete runs. Training, exploratory validation, official public scores and hidden/private evaluation are separate evidence levels.

An unfinished job is not a negative experiment. Passing software checks does not establish a quality gain. Selection on a validation set requires independent confirmation; repeated public-leaderboard queries are exploratory. Credit upstream code, models, datasets and public prediction libraries separately from our measured contribution.

Do not publish competition data, model weights, credentials or private operational records. Obtain allowed data from the official competition. Clinical benchmarks in these projects use public synthetic records and do not establish clinical safety or regulatory compliance.
